Infrastructure budget · options matrix

Updated September 24, 2026 · CAD before tax. The infrastructure budget now lives in one spreadsheet: 38 phase/provider options, their machine lists, GPU allocations, source-linked prices and phase calculations. These are proposed configurations, not verified deployed capacity.

Open the infrastructure options spreadsheet

Start on Infra Matrix. Filter by phase and provider, then select Machines → for the itemized configuration. Use Infra Phase Budget to select one option per phase and enter actual durations. This is a standalone workbook containing only the seven infrastructure tabs. The original Annex A financial workbook remains separate, with its financial sheets and comments preserved.

Choose the capacity you actually need

CloseoutArchive only; temporary export services plus archiveRelease GPUs after acceptance. Retain only the agreed evidence and recovery data.

Four families are not five independent jobs. Embeddings and reranking share the retrieval family. The four-family option alternates those two jobs; the five-job option allocates a GPU to each. The large language/vision candidates are alternatives: this does not mean four large LLMs must all be trained or hosted together. See the source-linked workload calculations and retrieval recipes.

The previous expanded configuration was not an all-model training fleet. The matrix retains it as option L-20 / T-20: one large training allocation plus two small serving allocations. Compare it with L-10 / T-10 for five training jobs and L-11 / T-11 for five training jobs plus independent small- and large-model serving.

Provider comparison and quote boundaries

Leaseweb uses the checked Montréal configurations: physical CPU and gateway hosts, L4 small-model GPUs, and an RTX PRO 6000 candidate whose exact 96 GB edition must be confirmed. Configurator links can open default selections; the workbook records the priced configuration. Backup object storage is separate.

ThinkOn uses public MSRP for L40S/H200 nodes and derived CPU/edge VM resource budgets. A100 and H100 alternatives are included with unresolved memory-variant warnings. No public-sector discount is assumed. ThinkOn's resource bundles are not equal-performance substitutes for Leaseweb's; vCPU GHz allocation, disk specifications, transit units and setup need confirmation.

A priced subtotal is not a complete supplier quote. Yellow blanks expose missing network/support/licence charges, HA storage and recovery, additional datasets/capture retention and setup where applicable. The 240 GB primary seed and roughly 479 GB for two recovery copies are bounded research quantities, not an allowance for unlimited production imagery. See the storage and service design.

Training counts describe GPU allocations, not proven throughput. Small-batch 24 GB recipes do not establish that every model/context fits, and separate serving GPUs do not prove a latency or HA target. Exact GPU editions, usable host RAM, residency, capacity, contract terms and the written bill of materials must be confirmed before procurement. Phase durations are inputs; no twelve-month commitment is assumed.
