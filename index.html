Current procurement position

Only Hypertec and Micrologic are pricing candidates. Their catalogues establish products and capabilities; the complete cloud bill remains quote required. Unknown rates are not zero. See the provider catalogue and phase quote requirements.

  • A: foundation and scheduled demo.
  • B: fine-tuning and evaluation on a shared worker.
  • C: production-shaped staging and failover rehearsal.
  • D: live production after capacity and reliability qualification.
  • E: separately priced geographic recovery.
  • F: optional distributed product expansion.
  • G: closeout, export and retained evidence.

A–D are complete operating states, not additive bills for the same month. E/F are incremental options. There is no assumed twelve-month project duration. Micrologic’s managed package commitment is a separate commercial constraint, described in the pricing guide.

Remaining evidence gates

  • Written supplier configurations, Canadian placement, availability, prices and terms.
  • Documents/pages per day and peak rate; retrieval QPS, depth and image mix; extraction context/concurrency/latency; capture frequency and retention.
  • Exact-board training, save/reload, serving, isolation and restore tests. These tests are not yet complete.
  • Phase durations and approved retention policies before a grand total can be calculated.
