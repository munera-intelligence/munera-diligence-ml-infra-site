Scope and budget rules

  • Pilot preparation/delivery: FY 2026–27 planning window; live testing: April–November 2027; closeout remains pilot work. Exact dates and acceptance require agreement. PSPC scope mapping.
  • Large-model adaptation is placed in post-pilot here. Move a specifically approved large-model pilot workload into its actual period; do not count it twice.
  • The platform runs applications, SQL metadata, Qdrant, Airflow/Temporal, MLflow, Git/DVC, S3 artifacts, CI/import, secrets and Prometheus/Grafana/logs within enforced boundaries. They are not separate physical machines by default. Service allocation.
  • Storage is case-based: ≈240 GB primary and ≈479 GB for two recovery points. Additional production documents, images and photogrammetry/BIM files require an ingest/retention forecast. No automatic 100 TB reservation.
  • Project total = the selected configuration’s rate × its actual non-overlapping duration + setup once + metered usage + quoted services. Reusing a node across phases does not create another setup fee. Phase worksheet.
  • Bare metal does not automatically reduce the GPU count. VRAM, model context, concurrency and measured throughput determine the selected capacity; validate exact models and failure handling before committing.
