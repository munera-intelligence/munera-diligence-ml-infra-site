What the two options include

Two-model training: one smaller model (classification, OCR, embeddings or reranking) plus one selected 24–32B language/vision model.

Four-model training: classification + OCR + one retrieval model + one selected large model. Embeddings and reranking alternate; training both simultaneously would be five jobs.

GPUCloud + WHC is one combined configuration: GPUCloud supplies GPU instances; WHC supplies platform/gateway hosts and a backup allowance. These are candidate allocations, not equal-performance substitutes or an approved private-cloud design. Separate serving allocations are included in the live/product phases.
