Shared infrastructure, isolation and hardware

Updated 24 September 2026. Published facts are linked inline. Calculated scenarios and planning assumptions require workload qualification; they are not measured capacity or approval.

B2 · Documented design and verified platform capabilities; not an implementation or security approval. The supplied Munera designs define the lab's service responsibilities. The four workload guides cover classification, document vision, retrieval and large models. Pricing records purchasable configurations separately from unmeasured workload requirements.

The telemetry caps have explicit example inputs: 10,000 series × 2 samples/30 seconds × 30 days × 2 bytes = 3.456 GB of compressed samples, within an 8 GiB allowance for WAL/head/overhead (Prometheus formula). 20 containers × 1 KiB/s × 7 days = 12.386 GB of raw logs; 25% format/index allowance fits 16 GiB. A TEE protects a different boundary from network policy; its presence does not establish an air gap. Published Anthropic sandboxing and OpenAI Codex isolation concern agent execution. Neither source validates Munera's GPU-training environment. Anthropic's later containment account reports exfiltration through an allowed API domain using attacker credentials. Its remediation scoped credentials and requests at the proxy. An allowed destination alone is therefore not the complete control described in that case.

The design's environment separation is now readable as explicit responsibilities, grounded in Kubernetes multi-tenancy guidance:

  • Identity: separate human and workload identities; least-privilege access per environment.
  • Execution: Dev, Staging and Production namespaces plus workload/process isolation; namespaces alone are not a hard tenant boundary.
  • Secrets and storage: distinct permissions and credentials for databases, buckets and registry paths; production credentials absent from experiments.
  • Network: deny by default; explicitly allow required internal services; controlled artifact import separate from model execution.
  • Evidence: test prohibited egress and allowed service paths; retain audit evidence and review changes. These tests have not been demonstrated here.

GPU allocation and omitted workload boundary

GPU allocation: one shared worker is a sequential training-lab starting point, not a serving-capacity proof. Small-family 24 GB qualification and the large-model alternatives are explicitly recipe-dependent. The large-model guide separates the selected mixed export, NF4 alternatives and PEFT FP32-upcast sensitivities; no device fits every possible preparation/context automatically. Additional devices may be needed for throughput, completion deadlines, failure isolation or uninterrupted serving.

Reconciliation: the inspected application contains 22 deterministic reconcilers (joins, dates, presence, thresholds and arithmetic), separate from GPU-backed extraction/retrieval. They require zero additional GPUs for those rules. Four product modules are not the same thing as four model families.

Reality capture: the internal design records a 322-photo RTK drone job through OpenDroneMap, generating mesh, orthophoto and point-cloud assets. This is a separate reconstruction workload, not evidence of neural-model training. ODM's published examples include 225- and 493-image jobs. Its sizing guidance gives 32 GB RAM around 500 images and recommends 100 GB disk for a few hundred images. Proposed starting allocation: 16 CPU equivalents, 64 GiB host RAM, 100 GB temporary workspace, scheduled on the existing training host while fine-tuning is idle. This is a conservative qualification proposal, not an observed Munera peak.

A CPU reconstruction route requires zero additional GPUs; current ODM supports optional CUDA feature extraction, which can share the existing GPU if qualified. Do not promise a complete reconstruction speed-up from a feature-stage speed-up. The published 7,917-image study used about 700 GB disk, but its fast-orthophoto workflow differs from our textured-mesh requirement; linear extrapolation is only a sensitivity, not a capacity rule.

Retained capture storage is capture count × (raw photos + retained point clouds + meshes/textures + orthophotos/DSM + metadata) + BIM versions; disposable reconstruction workspace is separate. Photo resolution, compressed bytes and capture frequency have not been established. Keep these additive to the 239.281 GB ML/platform scenario, not silently inside it. Budget boundary.

Partitioning, distributed training and controls

Optional GPU partitioning: NVIDIA MIG supports four nominal 24 GB or two 48 GB partitions on the specified RTX PRO 6000 variants. Each slice has only its share of compute; a fit is not full-board throughput. No claim that every small recipe fits is made. GPU Operator issue 2249 is a closed Max-Q/particular-version report, not a generic Server Edition blocker.

gVisor's current GPU guide does not establish RTX Blackwell qualification and excludes the MIG capability interfaces. Verify exact supported driver ABIs with the delivered runtime; no permanent numeric driver floor is inferred. For a TEE, verify the actual NVIDIA CPU/GPU/firmware/runtime matrix, attestation and egress policy. A chassis name does not establish firmware, attestation or TEE compatibility. Protect etcd storage from checkpoint contention and measure fsync latency.

Distributed communication: DDP synchronizes gradients, not all optimizer state. Ideal two-rank all-reduce of nominal 32B FP32 gradients moves 128 GB per rank: 102.4 seconds at raw 10 Gb/s; BF16 halves it. Rank-16 LoRA has 0.536870912 GB FP32 gradients, approximately 0.172 seconds at raw 25 Gb/s before protocol/collective overhead. These transfer bounds exclude collective overhead and must be compared with measured compute time; sharded training has a different communication pattern. Price a distributed fabric only after a justified job and benchmark.

Reality-capture qualification: retain the 322-photo scenario at 16 CPU equivalents, 64 GiB RAM and 100 GB scratch. ODM sizing guidance is workload-dependent; measure runtime and peak RAM at the selected resolution/options. Retained capture products are additional storage. Quote qualification labour in the pricing guide.
