Managed OpenShift is a separate production option

The March 2025 sheet requires three control, three infrastructure and at least three worker nodes, with a minimum one-year commitment. These are nine cluster nodes, not nine dedicated physical hosts.

  • Control: 3 × 8 vCPU / 16 GB / 100 GB disk.
  • Standard infrastructure: 3 × 4 vCPU / 16 GB / 200 GB disk.
  • Example workers: 3 × OSGv1c8m32, each 8 vCPU / 32 GB / 100 GB disk.
  • Derived total: 60 vCPU, 192 GB RAM, 1,200 GB node disk, before persistent volumes, registry and GPU workers.
  • NFS minimum: 2 TB per region / 1 TB per cluster; obtain billing interpretation in writing.
  • Quote licences, registry, application backup and activation; the sheet mentions both no initial fees and an activation item.

Those package minimums are commercial constraints, not evidence that model assets need terabytes. Compare the smaller IaaS route for the demo.

The 239.281 GB durable primary is spread across artifacts, SQL, registry and audit storage, not automatically one S3 bill. Quote placement and usable units explicitly. Under the local-primary scenario, the proposed 500 GB platform volume already contains it; do not add another 240 GB bill unless choosing a separate storage service. See the itemized disk and restore plan. Request 1 TB/5 TB growth unit prices as options only.

Cirrus S3 starts in 1 GB increments; Cirrus File Service has a 4 TB minimum quota. These are different products. S3 lists throughput up to 83 MB/s: confirm per-stream/bucket/tenant scope. Versioning/replication is not proof of immutable independent backups. Confirm replicated billing without counting included copies twice.

Capture bytes = captures × (photos + point clouds + meshes/textures + orthophotos + metadata) + BIM versions. With unknown capture frequency/file sizes, 100 TB is not justified, nor a blanket 1 TB per family.

Monthly OPEX = CPU + GPU + storage + backup + network + licences + support. Phase cost = rates × actual duration/usage + setup. Project total = non-overlapping phase costs, reconciled for shared resources and minimum commitments. CAPEX is separate cash expenditure; equipment MSRP cannot replace monthly cloud rates.

Dedicated/burst break-even hours = comparable monthly dedicated cost ÷ hourly all-in cost. Align hardware, CPU/RAM, storage, support and terms first. Bare metal does not inherently reduce required GPU count; model memory, throughput and concurrency determine it.
