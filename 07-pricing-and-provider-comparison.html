Infrastructure pricing — two or four models training simultaneously

Pricing basis: September 24, 2026 · CAD per month, before tax. This comparison includes the previously checked Leaseweb Montréal configurations and ThinkOn public MSRP, plus newly checked GPUCloud and Web Hosting Canada catalogue rates. No discount is assumed for ThinkOn. Each column is an alternative configuration: do not add vendors or alternatives together.

Two-model training: one smaller model (classification, OCR, embeddings or reranking) plus one selected 24–32B language/vision model. Four-model training: classification + OCR + one retrieval model + one selected large model. Embeddings and reranking alternate in the retrieval allocation; training both simultaneously would be five jobs and is outside these two choices.

Totals below add every priced line shown, including platform, gateway compute, storage and network allowances. They are not final all-in supplier quotes. Unquoted managed services, HA storage/recovery, licences, traffic overages and setup where unspecified remain extra. Model fit and concurrent throughput require qualification.

GPUCloud + Web Hosting Canada added September 24, 2026. These two providers form one proposed split-provider configuration: GPUCloud supplies GPU instances; WHC supplies platform/gateway hosts and a backup allowance. The two added columns retain the same phase roles and two-/four-job definitions. They are candidate allocations, not equivalent GPU performance or an approved private-cloud design.

Read the totals: “+ quote” means the displayed number is the sum of priced components only. Private networking between providers, additional GPU scratch disks, production storage/recovery and any required managed services remain unpriced. A missing price is not $0. WHC uses verified one-month checkout rates; GPUCloud uses its displayed monthly plan rates. No annual commitment or phase duration is assumed.

Hardware and network gaps to resolve before purchase:

  • Large model: the existing sizing calculation gives 68.345 GB for one mixed-dtype recipe but about 93.700 GB under FP32-upcast sensitivity. An 80 GB H100 cannot cover that second case. Qualify a lower-memory/QLoRA loader; otherwise require an available larger-memory SKU.
